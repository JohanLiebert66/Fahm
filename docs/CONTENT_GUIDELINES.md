# Fahm — Content Guidelines for Pack Authors

Fahm is used with children and with religious material. These are guidelines for people who make packs; the app does not enforce them.

## Religious texts (e.g. Quran)
- Use text from an established, verified source. Do not type it from memory and do not generate it with AI.
- Record the source and its license in `pack.json` (`source`, `license`).
- The app shows text exactly as stored and treats it as read-only. Teachers add explanations and images; they do not edit the text.
- Prefer sources that provide word-by-word splitting instead of guessing.

## Images
- Choose respectful, age-appropriate images. For religious content, take extra care with sacred figures and abstract concepts; prefer nature, objects, symbols and simple illustrations to depictions of people.
- AI-generated images must be reviewed by an adult before a child sees them.
- Only use images you made, own, or that are licensed for this use. Note the license in the pack.

## Audio
- Record in a quiet place. Short clips work best (a few seconds for pronunciation, under a minute for explanations).
- Only include recordings you made or have the right to share. Do not include other people's recitations without permission.

## Explanations
- Write in simple words for the target age. One idea per explanation, with a short example.
- For Arabic packs, give explanations in Arabic; add English if you want bilingual use.

## Children's privacy
- Progress data stays on the device. When sharing a pack or backup, remember that a full backup includes children's names and progress; share exported packs instead.
